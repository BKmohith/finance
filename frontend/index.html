TARGET PROBLEM STATEMENT: F-03

The 3 Fatal Traps for 30M Kiranas.

Small retailers don't fail from lack of hustle. They fail from silent margin leakage, invisible credit lockups, and disconnected payment soundboxes.

The Payment-Basket Gap

When a soundbox rings "Paytm pe ₹300 prapt hue", it emits zero SKU data. Did the customer buy 2 bottles of oil or clear previous Udhaar? Without itemization, FIFO inventory cannot decrement.

Silent Margin Erosion

Distributors print wholesale invoices on faded 9-pin dot-matrix paper. Cost price increases of 3% to 8% go unnoticed at morning rush, eroding retail profit margins before prices can be adjusted.

The 10:15 PM Blindspot

Store owners pull down shutters at night without knowing their real 14-day net cash position. Upcoming supplier cheque dates collide with uncollected customer Udhaar, causing severe liquidity crunches.
